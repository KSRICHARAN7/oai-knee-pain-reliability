# TRIPOD+AI reporting audit

Audit date: 2026-09-14
Guideline: Collins GS, Moons KGM, Dhiman P, et al. TRIPOD+AI statement. BMJ. 2024;385:e078378.

Scope:
- 52 TRIPOD+AI checklist subitems.
- 13-item TRIPOD+AI for Abstracts checklist.
- The audit was used only to improve reporting of the already frozen study.
- No cohort rule, predictor, outcome, model, threshold, hypothesis, or numerical result was changed.

Key technical additions:
- Supplementary Table S6: frozen predictor/outcome definitions.
- Supplementary Table S7: fold-level development/evaluation counts and selected elastic-net hyperparameters.
- Expanded reporting of data provenance, OAI dates/sites, outcome assessment, preprocessing, tuning grid, fairness approach, open-science status, and limitations.

Items intentionally left for publication-compliance work:
- Author funding declaration.
- Author conflict-of-interest declaration.
- Target-journal-specific ethics/IRB/exemption wording.
- Final code/repository access statement.

Transparent limitations rather than post-freeze analyses:
- Center-level performance heterogeneity was not evaluated.
- No single post-freeze full-cohort deployable model was fitted.
- Public documentation reviewed did not provide radiograph-reader demographics.
