# Final analysis freeze declaration

Freeze date: 2026-09-13
Status: FINAL ANALYSIS FREEZE

The OAI structure-pain discordance study is now analytically frozen.

Frozen components include:
- The baseline cohort construction and analysis-ready dataset used for the completed study.
- The primary exposure definition and cross-fitted discordance construction.
- The elastic-net primary prediction model, predictor set, preprocessing rules, participant-grouped nested five-fold validation design, and frozen seed.
- The primary 48-month discordance-to-held-out-error hypothesis test and all primary performance metrics.
- The completed 24/36/48-month longitudinal analysis.
- The nested uncertainty model, risk-coverage analysis, and prespecified 5%, 10%, 15%, and 20% abstention thresholds.
- The subgroup and abstention audit.
- The seven prespecified sensitivity analyses.
- The follow-up/attrition analysis.
- The knee-replacement feasibility decision that no defensible time-to-event model could be fit from the archived derived data.
- All numerical outputs and publication assets generated from these analyses.

Post-freeze policy:
1. No completed model, cohort rule, predictor set, outcome definition, threshold, hypothesis, or numerical result may be changed because of manuscript drafting, journal selection, physician review, or preference for a different result.
2. Corrections are allowed only for a verified coding, data-processing, transcription, or methodological error. Any such correction must be documented as a post-freeze correction with the reason, affected files, old value, new value, and date.
3. Any new analysis requested after this freeze must be labeled post-hoc/exploratory or treated as a separate external-replication project. It cannot silently replace a frozen confirmatory or prespecified result.
4. Manuscript wording, interpretation, references, formatting, and reporting-guideline compliance may continue to evolve without altering the frozen analysis.
5. Physician review may change clinical interpretation or wording but not the frozen analysis unless it reveals a genuine methodological error.

The original preregistration lock remains unchanged.
