# Literature and novelty audit specification

Date: 2026-09-13

Purpose:
- Establish what parts of the completed OAI project are already represented in prior literature.
- Identify the narrowest defensible novelty claim before drafting the Discussion.
- Prevent broad or unsupported 'first' claims.

Search domains:
1. Longitudinal OAI/FNIH pain prediction.
2. Structure-pain and radiograph-pain discordance.
3. Residual-based discordance definitions.
4. Uncertainty quantification, selective prediction, abstention, and risk-coverage analysis in knee OA.
5. Subgroup performance/coverage auditing.

Evidence hierarchy:
- Peer-reviewed primary studies and systematic reviews are preferred.
- Recent preprints are included only when they materially constrain novelty and are labeled as preprints.
- Novelty is assessed against the specific methodological combination, not against isolated components.

Stopping rule:
The audit is considered sufficient for manuscript drafting when searches repeatedly recover the same major pain-prediction, discordance, and uncertainty families and no directly matching study is identified. The audit is not a formal systematic review and cannot prove absolute worldwide novelty.
