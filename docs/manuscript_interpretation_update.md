# Manuscript interpretation update - October 14, 2026

The original supplied technical ZIP contains the frozen unadjusted primary analysis and seven sensitivity analyses. The separately supplied manuscript also reports a post-freeze baseline-pain-adjustment sensitivity analysis (S8). This public communication update incorporates that qualification without changing frozen code or numerical tables.

| Error-regression specification | Discordance coefficient | 95% CI | p value |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Frozen unadjusted primary | 0.301 | 0.275 to 0.326 | <0.001 |
| Post-freeze baseline-pain adjustment | -0.011 | -0.043 to 0.022 | 0.52 |
| Post-freeze baseline pain + KL adjustment | -0.020 | -0.053 to 0.012 | 0.22 |

The manuscript reports correlation of 0.751 between baseline pain and absolute discordance, and R-squared of 0.202 for baseline pain alone versus 0.111 for discordance alone in explaining absolute error. The adjusted association was not statistically distinguishable from zero. An independent discordance effect is not established by these data. The adjustments are post hoc and should not be described as prespecified confirmatory analyses.

The uncertainty model included discordance; its performance is not independent verification of a discordance effect. Retained-case MAE reflects a coverage tradeoff.
